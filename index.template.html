Change one property, change the technique

      
Each card runs the same engine twice (or three times) on requests that differ in one property only. The framework's choice, and the reason for it, follows the property.

      

    


    

      
Stress-test lab

      
Every prescribed topic is tested on a generated family of inputs. Measured operation counts are plotted on a logarithmic axis against the theoretical bound, then rated Low, Moderate or High risk.

      

      
Risk summary

        
Test familyTheoretical boundObservedRisk

        
Low: practical for the tested sizes and matches the expected growth. Moderate: growth is significant, so input limits or alternatives should be considered. High: the exact approach becomes impractical or resource use grows severely.
